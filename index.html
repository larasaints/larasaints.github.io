UrbanBite

Multi-Zap Franchise Operations & Compliance Automation Engine

The Problem

Managing franchise operations across multiple locations manually leads to operational bottlenecks. Inspection results can be difficult to track, critical business permits can approach expiration unnoticed, and missing monthly reports require compliance teams to manually cross-reference records and follow up with operators.

The Solution

A centralized, multi-Zap automated operations architecture that manages the franchise compliance lifecycle. The system captures form submissions, updates tracking databases, monitors deadlines using scheduled workflows, triggers operational escalations, and feeds status metrics into a centralized operations dashboard.

Ecosystem Architecture
01
New Compliance Submission
Captures submissions, creates records, identifies issues, and triggers alerts.
02
Issue Resolution & Corrective Action

03
Overdue Issue Monitoring & Escalation
Identifies overdue issues and sends consolidated escalation reports.
04
Document Expiration Monitoring
Identifies expired and upcoming documents requiring renewal.
05
Missing Monthly Compliance Submission
Checks active franchises and sends reminders while preventing duplicate alerts.

AI Automation • n8n Gemini AI

AI Customer Support Automation

Intelligent Ticket Triage Engine

The Problem

Customer support teams often spend significant time manually reading incoming requests, categorizing issues, creating tickets, drafting responses, and identifying cases that require escalation.

The Solution

Built an AI-assisted support intake and triage workflow using n8n and Google Gemini. The system receives customer messages through a webhook, checks for duplicates, classifies the request, generates a draft response, logs structured ticket data in Airtable, and routes sensitive or uncertain requests for human review.

AI & Automation Logic
  • ✓ Classifies Billing, Access, Scheduling, Technical Support, General Question, and Refund Request.
  • ✓ Generates confidence scores and classification reasons.
  • ✓ Creates AI-generated customer response drafts.
  • ✓ Escalates refund requests and low-confidence cases.
  • ✓ Sends Gmail notifications for human review.
  • ✓ Tracks ticket status and processing timestamps.
  • ✓ Includes dedicated n8n error handling.
  • ✓ Prevents duplicate processing through Message ID validation.
Key Engineering Metrics
Event-driven webhook AI classification Human-review rules Airtable ticket logging Duplicate prevention Error handling

THE KITCHEN DECK

Mobile-Responsive Web Application, Production-Grade Product

The Problem

Traditional sports training regimens suffer from low compliance due to two primary friction points.

01
Cognitive Load on Court
Players must remember complex drill layouts, scoring rules, and target zones while physically exerting themselves.
02
Lack of Feedback Loops
Static drill sheets offer no immediate psychological reward, leading to rapid dropout rates and unstructured practice sessions.
The Solution

A high-contrast, touch-optimized visual engine that translates structural sports drills into gamified digital cards.

01
Visual Frameworks
Breaks down spatial movement patterns into glanceable, high-fidelity card layouts.
02
Behavioral Mechanics
Replaces mental tracking with real-time feedback loops, XP counters, and level progression models to increase user engagement.
Core & Architecture

The underlying source code, database structures, custom visual card templates, and corporate branding for this project are proprietary and hosted in a private repository to protect intellectual property.
